# SkillFlow AI One-Pager

## Positioning

SkillFlow AI is a Spanish-first SaaS HRTech platform for training management, compliance readiness, and digital certification across Chile and LatAm.

## Problem

Companies, OTEC providers, and HR teams often manage training operations through spreadsheets, emails, disconnected attendance records, and manual certificate evidence. This creates low executive visibility, weak audit readiness, and operational friction when demonstrating compliance.

## Solution

SkillFlow AI centralizes the training lifecycle:

- Employee profiles
- Course catalog
- Training sessions
- Enrollments
- Attendance evidence
- Evaluations
- Digital certificates
- SENCE readiness evidence

The current product does not claim official SENCE submission automation. It prepares evidence and workflow visibility for internal review and commercial demos.

## Market

Initial target segments:

- Chilean and LatAm enterprise HR teams
- Mining, industrial, construction, retail, and services companies
- OTEC and OTIC organizations
- Companies seeking structured training evidence
- Seed investors evaluating SaaS HRTech opportunities
