# SkillFlow AI 7-Minute Demo Script

## Audience

This script is designed for:

- HR managers
- Operations managers
- OTEC stakeholders
- Investors

## Minute 1: Positioning And Login

Open with the core message:

> SkillFlow AI helps companies manage training, compliance readiness, and digital certification in one Spanish-first SaaS platform.

Show the public landing page and log in with the demo credentials:

```text
Email: admin@skillflow.demo
Password: DemoPassword123
```

## Minute 2: Executive Dashboard

Show the executive dashboard first.

Highlight:

- Training compliance
- PAC progress
- Average attendance
- Certificates issued
- Compliance alerts
- SENCE status
- Audit and traceability signals

Message for HR and operations:

> The dashboard converts operational training data into executive visibility.

## Minute 3: Employees And Critical Courses

Open Employees and Courses.

Highlight:

- Employee records by area and role
- Critical course catalog
- Active courses for planning
- Search and filter behavior

Message for enterprise buyers:

> The platform creates a single operational source of truth for training demand and workforce readiness.

## Minute 4: Sessions And Enrollments

Open Training Sessions and Enrollments.

Highlight:

- Session capacity
- Schedule readiness
- Participant enrollment
- Status tracking

Message for OTEC:

> This flow reduces manual coordination across participants, courses, and scheduled training delivery.

## Minute 5: Attendance And Evaluations

Open Attendance and Evaluations.

Highlight:

- Attendance evidence
- Manual bulk attendance demo
- Knowledge checks
- Passing score thresholds

Message for operations:

> Attendance and evaluation records become structured evidence for completion and certification.

## Minute 6: Certificates

Open Certificates.

Highlight:

- Certificate preview
- Employee and course context
- Certificate number
- Verification code
- Professional QR placeholder

Message for HR and auditors:

> Certificates are not just records; they are verifiable evidence connected to training execution.

## Minute 7: SENCE Readiness And Close

Open SENCE.

Highlight:

- Evidence checklist
- Session registered
- Participants enrolled
- Attendance registered
- Evaluations available
- Certificates issued
- Declaration readiness

State the boundary clearly:

> This is a SENCE evidence preparation workflow. It does not represent automatic official submission to SENCE.

Close with:

> SkillFlow AI is ready for pilots with companies, OTEC providers, and HR teams that need better visibility, evidence, and certification traceability.
