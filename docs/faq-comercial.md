# SkillFlow AI Commercial FAQ

## What is SkillFlow AI?

SkillFlow AI is a Spanish-first SaaS HRTech platform for managing training, compliance readiness, attendance evidence, evaluations, and digital certificates.

## Does SkillFlow AI integrate with SENCE?

Not yet. The current product provides SENCE readiness workflows and evidence preparation. It does not perform official automatic submission to SENCE.

## Is SkillFlow AI a SaaS product?

Yes. The current architecture is deployed as a SaaS stack with frontend on Vercel, backend on Render, and PostgreSQL on Supabase.

## Can it operate multiple companies?

The backend supports organization-scoped data and tenant-aware records. Commercial multi-company operations should be validated during pilot configuration.

## Does it provide traceability?

Yes. The product connects employees, courses, sessions, enrollments, attendance, evaluations, certificates, and SENCE readiness records. The backend also includes audit-oriented infrastructure.

## Does it provide digital certification?

Yes. Certificates include certificate number, verification code, employee, course, session, issue date, status, and a professional visual preview. PDF and real QR generation are not part of the current demo.

## Does SkillFlow AI have APIs?

Yes. The backend exposes REST endpoints for authentication, organizations, users, employees, courses, training plans, sessions, enrollments, attendance, evaluations, certificates, and SENCE declarations.

## Does SkillFlow AI have AI?

Not in the current demo. AI is part of the roadmap for gap detection, advanced analytics, and HR Copilot capabilities. No real AI Copilot is implemented in the current version.

## Who is the first buyer?

The strongest early buyers are HR teams, training operations teams, OTEC providers, OTIC stakeholders, and companies in mining, industrial, construction, services, and retail sectors.

## What is the best demo path?

Use the 7-minute flow:

```text
Executive Dashboard -> Employees -> Courses -> Sessions -> Attendance
-> Evaluations -> Certificates -> SENCE
```
